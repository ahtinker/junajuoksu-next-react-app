## 1\. General information  
  
Personal information or personal data is information that can be linked in one way or another, directly or indirectly, to a living human being. The processing of personal data is fundamentally linked to the collection, transmission and storage of data. Examples of personal data and information that can be linked to a living person include email address, telephone number, name and home address. Cookies are data that is stored locally on a person's device. Data controller means the party responsible for and in charge of the processing of personal data. Personal data holder or owner means the service user whose personal data is processed.  
  
The controller of Junajuoksu is Ahti Ankeriasniemi ahti.ankeriasniemi@junajuoksu.fi

## 2\. How we process personal data  
  
The processing of personal data starts from the moment the person has given their consent to the processing of the data. We process the personal data mentioned below:

*   The exact geographical location of the device
*   The physical speed of the device
*   Trains that the person travels with and has travelled with
*   Helsinki regional transport busses that the person travels with and has travelled with
*   IP address of the device's network
*   Email address, profile picture, first and last name associated with Google Account
*   All data that the user has manually inputted 

The data will remain in the database of Junajuoksu. We do not transmit or monetize personal data to any third party outside this service’s providers, unless the receiver is not mentioned in this policy. However, personal data may be included in large surveys, statistics or other larger aggregations where personal data cannot be directly linked to a living human being. Personal data may also be transmitted to authorities if necessary.  

The Service does not automatically send emails for marketing.

To use the account feature of the Service, the Service may require your email address, full name and Google account profile picture. Your email address will be used by workplaces to send you invites to their groups. If necessary, developers of the Service may contact you via your email address.
  
The service is built on the [Railway](https://railway.app/) service.

## 3\. Aggregation of personal data  
  
We link personal data back to their owners using cookies. A unique identifier for the person is written in the user's cookie, which is later used to retrieve personal data from the database. The identifier (“Token”) is at least 40 characters long and extremely difficult to guess. That token is associated with another similar string of characters, called a public identifier which anyone can see. The public identifier cannot be used to retrieve any other data of you than:

*   Your TrainChat messages
*   Your username
*   Public transport trips that you have verified your presence on and the time of verification. Any individual who wants to know which trips you have been on cannot retrieve a complete list of the trips that you have been on, but instead go through every single trip ever done in Finland and then ask the server if you have been on that single trip. 

Here is an example of a unique identifier: ```11542603202-ae3b9d67-d0dd-4110-906c-d02aad86a953```

## 4\. Target group  
  
Target group is meant to point to a living group of human beings whose personal data we process.  
  
We process the personal data of the owner of a person who is located in Finland, travels or has travelled by train or Helsinki regional transport's busses and has given permission to process their train or bus information via the junajuoksu.fi service. A person under the age of 13 must request permission to use the service from their parent or guardian responsible for using the service.

## 5\. Managing and observing your personal data  
  
You can influence how your personal data is processed, see what personal data we have stored about you and if necessary delete any data associated with you [here](/privacy-dashboard). Not all data about you is shown on that site. However, you may request a complete list of your data by sending an email to support@junajuoksu.fi.   

If you decide to delete all information associated with you, the deletion of all information may take up to 30 days. The delay in deleting the information applies to your IP address so that we can associate any inappropriate TrainChat messages with you before the information is deleted.

In case you have logged in to the Service with your Google account and you want to completely delete your Junajuoksu account, you must contact our customer service at support@junajuoksu.fi.
